Money keeps leaving, whales keep shorting: Bitcoin sat just under $76,000 on Sunday morning, about 2.7% lower on the week, as the market heads into a weekly close that could make five red weeks in a row. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.28B to outflows from Nov 2 to 6, and ether funds about $365M more.

Glassnode says big Hyperliquid traders now hold their largest net bitcoin short since October 2025, and Abraxas Capital alone carries about $452M of shorts. Against that, a wallet that had been quiet for more than a year picked up about 3,009 BTC, roughly $228M, on Friday.

Altcoins had a rough week: NEAR fell about 15% over seven days to near $3.71. US inflation data arrives Tuesday, Nov 10. Fresh searches again found no new large HBAR or TAO wallet moves.
