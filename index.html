Quiet Friday close: Bitcoin is holding near $81,400, a little lower on the day and down about 1.5% for the week, its second weekly loss in a row. Ether has climbed back above $2,400 to roughly $2,430. Hot September inflation readings (consumer prices up 3.7% from a year ago, producer prices up 6.3%) still hang over the market, though Fed Chair Kevin Warsh did not signal another hike on Friday and odds of an October increase fell to about 15%. Spot Bitcoin funds saw about $157M leave this week and Ether funds about $250M.

On the whale side, CryptoQuant says newer large Bitcoin holders now make up about a third of all whale cost basis, and their average buy-in sits near $77,000, a level worth watching if prices slide. On Hyperliquid, big traders still hold more shorts than longs, and the much-watched 5x Ether short has cut its paper loss to about $10.4M as Ether stalled.

No large HBAR or TAO whale moves turned up in reporting tonight; both coins slipped slightly with the market.
