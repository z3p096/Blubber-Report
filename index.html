A 2013 wallet stirs: A Bitcoin holder who got coins in 2013, when BTC traded near $131, moved 411.45 BTC worth about $34M to a fresh address on Sunday, Arkham data shows. The coins did not go to an exchange, so it looks more like a wallet upgrade than a sale. Separately, wallets tied to miner MARA have sent about 1,000 BTC to Binance and Gate over five days, Lookonchain says.

Bitcoin pushed toward $83,000 early Monday as cheaper oil and stronger Chinese growth figures lifted stocks and crypto alike. It was near $82,850, up about 0.8%, while Ether rose about 0.5% to around $2,438, enough to force-close another slice of a big Hyperliquid ETH short.

The rest of the market is mixed. TAO and NEAR are both down about 6%, and HBAR is flat near $0.083; fresh searches found no large new HBAR or TAO whale moves. US spot Bitcoin funds have still lost about $1B over two weeks, so the mood is cautious rather than bullish.
