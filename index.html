Big coins head back to the exchanges: A Bitcoin whale flagged by Onchain Lens sent about 949.7 BTC (roughly $77.6M) to Binance and Deribit early Friday, likely to sell, and parked another ~4,150 BTC in a wallet that has fed exchanges before. That lands right after U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs lost about $635.5M on Thursday. Bitcoin is holding near $81,500, just above the $80,000–$81,000 floor traders are watching, while oil above $100 and a weak Nasdaq keep risk appetite thin.

Deeper on-chain, the picture is calmer. CryptoQuant counts whale holdings (excluding exchanges and miners) back at about 3.03M BTC, the most since November 2025, and fresh wallets keep pulling Bitcoin out of Wintermute. Ether is flat near $2,400 as the BTC-to-ETH rotation whale keeps buying and early holders keep trimming.

Separate searches for HBAR and TAO again found no new large whale moves. TAO is the day's standout, up about 2%, and HBAR is slightly lower near $0.08.
