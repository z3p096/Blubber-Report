Big wallets stay short into the vote: CoinGlass data cited by U.Today shows Hyperliquid wallets of $1 million or more holding about $3.08 billion short against $1.46 billion long, and a wider Coinglass count in ChainCatcher puts whales at about $4.04 billion short and $3.48 billion long. The loudest exception is an unnamed wallet long 35.03 million XRP, about $44 million, nearly 80% of all large-wallet XRP longs on the venue. AguilaTrades, down about $38.2 million since last June, has just added margin to a bitcoin short of about $9.35 million. And analyst Yu Jin tracks a whale that sold ether at a profit of about $8.99 million two weeks ago and now holds a crude oil long of about $148 million.

Binance is the other story. It logged $10.97 billion of withdrawals and $5.28 billion of net outflows over 46 hours, though by 03:00 UTC on Nov 3 hourly deposits had moved ahead of withdrawals. CryptoQuant contributor Gaah counts about 28,805 BTC out, about 4.35% of its bitcoin reserves, and sees no bank run. Changpeng Zhao cites reports that only 6% to 10% of withdrawals went to other exchanges. Coins also keep moving to unknown wallets: about 2 million SOL left Binance on Nov 1, and 1,163 BTC, about $89.4 million, left OKX. Whale Alert flagged 40 million XRP, about $50 million, moving out of a wallet labeled as the Binance hacker's. Funds are shedding too, with US spot bitcoin ETFs down $398.6 million on Nov 2 and ether ETFs down $139.8 million.

The mood is fearful on US midterm election day, with the Fear and Greed index at 26. CoinDesk says bitcoin is on course for a fifth straight weekly loss, with oil rebounding and an Apple warning on chip costs adding pressure. Even so, prices are steadier this morning, with bitcoin near $77,618 and XRP leading our eight, up about 2.3%. NEAR is weakest, down about 6.6%, in a selloff of AI tokens. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no large wallet moves; the nearest HBAR signal is Binance top-trader accounts sitting about 60.8% long.
