Big traders turn defensive: A whale that had ridden bitcoin and ether longs since July closed about $100M of them for a profit near $17.8M after the Binance theft, then opened a 20,000 ETH short, EmberCN reports. Across Hyperliquid, large shorts still outweigh large longs, roughly $4.33B to $3.72B.

Binance, meanwhile, says its 7,499 BTC SAFU transfer is done, part of a $6.34B refill of its user fund pool before withdrawals restart on Nov 1. Some buyers are still pulling coins off exchanges, including 1,199 BTC from OKX, though OKX noted that a separate "unknown wallet" transfer flagged by trackers was its own internal move, a reminder that unlabeled wallets are not always new owners.

Prices are steady to slightly higher, with bitcoin near $77,400 and ether near $2,250, and QNT leading the group with a gain of about 5%. Strive CEO Matt Cole says bitcoin has held up better than expected but it is too early to call a bottom. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
