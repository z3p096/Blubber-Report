Midday reversal: the morning bounce faded. Bitcoin slipped back below $81,500 as oil climbed and AI-linked tech stocks sold off, and Ether gave back its overnight gain of about 5%. Liquidations stayed calm, at about $290M market-wide versus $929M the day before.

Earlier, Ether had jumped as whales bought the pullback and hotter inflation reports failed to knock crypto down. The Bitcoin-for-Ether rotation is still the week's main theme.

Leverage is the risk to watch: one trader opened $187M of max-leverage longs with a Bitcoin liquidation line near $80,600. CryptoQuant says big Bitcoin wallets hold 3.03M BTC, the most since November 2025, and that newer whales, now a third of that group by cost, bought in near $77,000 on average.
