Shorts get squeezed: Bitcoin pushed through $84,000 on Wednesday, trading near $84,400 and up about 2.5% on the day, while Ether climbed to about $2,510. Coinglass data cited by Bitcoin.com shows roughly $188M of short bets were forced closed in 24 hours, and traders are still eyeing the Fed's Oct. 27–28 meeting, where markets expect no rate change.

Whales are leaning into the bounce in different ways. The UBTC-to-ETH swapper keeps turning Bitcoin into Ether, now more than 46,100 ETH since Oct. 11, while a large BTC short was partly liquidated and sits just under its liquidation price. CryptoQuant still flags heavy whale deposits to exchanges, so not every big holder is buying the rally.

XRP leads the majors, up about 4.6% ahead of an XRP Ledger upgrade on Oct. 23, and Solana is up about 2.7% as its Alpenglow upgrade goes live. HBAR is up about 2% and TAO almost 4%; separate searches found no large new HBAR or TAO whale moves.
