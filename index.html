The drain keeps growing: DefiLlama now shows about $5.8 billion in net outflows from Binance over the past 24 hours, according to a Cointelegraph report carried by ChainCatcher. Binance's own count is smaller, about $3.6 billion net since withdrawals reopened, with roughly $4.6 billion going out against about $1 billion coming in. The exchange says that is a small slice of about $171 billion in customer assets and that withdrawals have slowed.

The two numbers cover different time windows, so they do not contradict each other. Some of the money is moving to rivals such as OKX and Bybit, while the hackers keep pushing stolen coins through Tron and cross-chain bridges.

Prices are calm. Bitcoin sits near $78,000 and ether near $2,260, both up less than 1%. NEAR is the weak spot, down about 4%, and fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no new large whale moves.
