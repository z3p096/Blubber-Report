Quiet evening before CPI: Bitcoin is sitting near $74,900 on Monday evening, Pacific time, slightly lower over 24 hours after bouncing off an intraday low of about $73,670, its weakest level in roughly two months. Ether is near $2,121, XRP about $1.10 and SOL around $86. Stocks rallied hard on Monday, but crypto barely moved with them, and about $1.2B of mostly long positions were wiped out over the past day.

The whale picture is unchanged from this afternoon: BlackRock moved about $483M of BTC and ETH to Coinbase, roughly $900M of XRP landed on Binance, and a UXLINK-linked wallet is unwinding $148M of longs, while ether dip-buyers keep pulling coins off exchanges. Fresh searches found no new large moves in the last few hours, and again none verified for HBAR or TAO.

October inflation data lands Tuesday at 5:30 a.m. PT, and traders put the odds of a December rate hike near 80%. Expect choppy prices around the release.
