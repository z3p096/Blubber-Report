Whales step back from Binance: CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost finds big holders sent about $3.8 billion of Bitcoin to Binance over the past 30 days, down 36% since late August, while smaller traders' deposits nearly doubled to $10.75 billion, a six-month high. For now, most of the coins that could be sold are coming from retail, not whales.

Bitcoin sits near $79,600 on Sunday night, down about 1.7%, and Ether is near $2,300, down 2.5%. Traders are waiting on Wednesday's Fed decision, where FedWatch puts the odds of a hold at about 91%, and on roughly $11 billion of Bitcoin options expiring Friday. Coinglass counted about $120 million of liquidations in a day, mostly longs, while Hyperliquid whales still lean slightly short.

Separately, an institution that backed Aave and Worldcoin moved about $45 million of tokens, including 6,000 ETH, to exchanges. NEAR and QNT edge higher while most majors slip. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
