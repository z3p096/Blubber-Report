Coins leave exchanges while traders bet against them: CryptoQuant counted about 12,570 BTC (~$1.01B) more leaving exchanges than arriving on Tuesday, one of October's biggest daily outflows and a sharp turn from the prior day's net inflow. Most of that comes from a single wallet that pulled 11,330 BTC off Binance, which usually points to holding rather than selling.

Leveraged traders tell a different story. Glassnode says Hyperliquid's largest Bitcoin traders are net short for the first time since March, and TokenPost's snapshot of the biggest Hyperliquid accounts shows about $4.33B in shorts against $3.76B in longs. Meanwhile long-idle wallets, a MARA-linked address and BlackRock's IBIT wallet all sent Bitcoin to trading desks, and a longtime Ether holder moved 3,084 ETH to Binance.

Prices kept climbing into the evening: Bitcoin is back above $81,000 and Ether is near $2,360, with XRP leading the large coins, as the Fed's two-day meeting gets under way. QNT is still down about 6%. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again found no fresh large whale moves.
