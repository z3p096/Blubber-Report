Binance hackers nearly empty their SOL stash as bitcoin holds above $80,000: Elliptic's latest count has about 725,000 SOL, about $67M, leaving the theft wallet in under 18 hours, with only about 56,000 SOL left behind. It still sees about $3.21 billion in addresses tied to the theft, and says the 18,270 stolen BTC has not moved. Bitcoin is near $80,651 on CoinGecko, up 4.3% in a day, and ether is near $2,421, up 5.4%.

The leverage fight is still lopsided. Coinglass data in ChainCatcher had Hyperliquid whales at about $4.10 billion short against $3.62 billion long on Nov. 4, and Nexo told The Block about $469M of leveraged positions were flushed in the rebound. One address, 0x5b5d, was showing a $5.4M paper gain on a 5x ether short from $2,322.71; by our arithmetic ether now trades above that entry. Onchain Lens also saw a whale pull about 303.86 BTC, about $23.4M, off Binance and split it across wallets.

Caution remains. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $643M, $399M and $116M in the last three reported sessions, with Nov. 4 data not yet out, even as Strategy added 811 BTC. Ether ETFs lost about $85M more on Nov. 3. CryptoQuant data in CryptoSlate shows past post-midterm drawdowns of 18.5% to 49.7%, and October jobs data lands Nov. 6. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no large wallet moves. The closest signals are a TAO Synergies filing that CoinStats says lifts its holdings above 54,000 TAO, and Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
