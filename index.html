Capitulation Saturday: Bitcoin kept sliding through the morning and briefly touched $65,000, a three-month low, and is down about 5% on the day near $65,100. Ether is near $1,815, XRP about $0.95 and SOL fell about 9% to roughly $72. Analyst James Check calls it the fourth capitulation since the all-time high, with buyers from the $80,000s in September now selling, though he also calls this a deep-value zone. Long liquidations over the past day ran to roughly $280M on the big exchanges.

Whales are split on ether. Wallets linked to ShapeShift founder Erik Voorhees have bought about $97M of ETH over four days, and another buyer added about $20M, while one large holder dumped 16,700 ETH at about $1,852 and the Ethereum Foundation-linked wallet kept selling. On Hyperliquid, the biggest traders now lean net short, with shorts making up about 55% of $5.1B in whale positions.

TAO lost its lone-gainer status, falling about 10% to near $192, and NEAR dropped about 11%. HBAR sits under six cents. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no fresh, verified whale moves, and no 24h HBAR change was found in a current source (the live feed fills it in).
