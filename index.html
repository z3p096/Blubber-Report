Bitcoin climbs back to $78,000 after the Democratic sweep: NBC News projected Democrats will control both the House and Senate, crypto.news reports, though some races were still being counted. Bitcoin dipped to about $75,700 on the news, then recovered. TokenPost had it at $78,016 on Binance at 2 a.m. ET, up 2.43% in four hours, with ether near $2,295. Traders quoted by CoinDesk say the drop faded because the uncertainty ended, and that Polymarket had priced a Democratic win for some time. The rebound hurt shorts: Lookonchain saw one address liquidated six times in two hours, about $15.45 million in all.

The largest fresh wallet move is 1,073.49 BTC, about $83.39 million, sent to a new wallet, per Onchain Lens. On Hyperliquid, Coinglass figures still show whales leaning short, about $4.10 billion against $3.62 billion long. The Binance hacker is the other big story: Lookonchain counted 970,522 SOL, about $95.9 million, in attempted conversions to BTC on Nov 1, while CryptoTicker's ledger check shows 233.46 million stolen XRP sitting unspent in six new wallets.

The mood is cautious but improving. Fear and Greed sits at 34, up from 26, and SoSoValue shows bitcoin ETF outflows shrinking from $643.3 million on Oct 30 to $115.56 million on Nov 3. Binance is still down on the week: KuCoin, citing DefiLlama, shows about $1.6 billion of 24-hour net inflows against about $11.9 billion of seven-day net outflows. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
