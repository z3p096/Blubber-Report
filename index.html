Still sliding: Bitcoin dipped to about $67,900 overnight and was trading near $69,000 early Friday UTC, down about 6% on the day. Ether sits near $1,940, and XRP briefly slipped under $1 before recovering to about $1.03. The Fear and Greed index fell to 20, its lowest since early July, as a hot October inflation print keeps a December Fed hike in play, the dollar index holds above 103 and the 10-year Treasury yield sits above 5%.

Leverage took the worst of it again: CoinGlass counts roughly $1.3B to $1.4B of liquidations in 24 hours, nearly all of them longs, and Lookonchain says four whales alone lost about $144M of positions. Order-flow data from Alphractal shows the biggest orders leaning short while small traders keep buying, and US spot bitcoin ETFs have shed about $1.44B over five sessions.

Big ether holders are selling into the low, with one whale dumping 16,700 ETH near $1,852 and an Ethereum Foundation-linked wallet selling 2,000 more. XRP whales were adding earlier in the week. Separate searches again found no fresh, verified HBAR or TAO whale moves.
