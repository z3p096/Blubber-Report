The big dip buyer may not be a buyer: Lookonchain now says the address behind the 27,553 ETH purchase, about $63 million, is suspected of being linked to a theft. It had first described the address as a whale. ZachXBT says an unnamed EU entity appears to have lost more than $106 million from exchange accounts, with stolen USDC swapped into ETH and much of it moved on toward Monero. The link is not confirmed, but it changes how one of the weekend's biggest buying stories reads.

Large traders still lean toward lower prices. Coinglass shows Hyperliquid whales holding about $4.15 billion in shorts against $3.59 billion in longs, and two wallets alone account for about $1.06 billion of short bets. Elsewhere, a wallet quiet for more than six years moved 999.953 BTC, which is a transfer and not a confirmed sale.

The mood is cautious before Tuesday's US midterms, with bitcoin near $78,000, ether near $2,285 and the Fear and Greed gauge last at 37. Binance said Monday its reserve proof for the Oct 31 snapshot is ready and about 95% of affected assets have been replaced, with more withdrawals due back from Tuesday. Last week's spot funds lost about $432 million in ether, $203 million in bitcoin and $71 million in Solana, while XRP took in about $4 million. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
