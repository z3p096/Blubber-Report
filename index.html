Shaky Sunday: Bitcoin dipped to about $61,950 on Coinbase overnight, its lowest in months, then bounced back to around $64,500. That still leaves it down about 4% on the day and more than 13% on the week. Ether sits near $1,790, SOL near $70 and XRP near $0.96. Liquidations topped $2B over 24 hours, mostly longs, and the Fear and Greed index is stuck at 10.

Whales are not running for the exits together. Lookonchain counts about 95,000 ETH bought by Voorhees-linked wallets and 7 Siblings, and two more big wallets pulled over $40M of ETH off Binance and OKX. On Hyperliquid, one very profitable trader kept adding to a 200 BTC long, while another is nursing a $10.5M ETH loss after switching into SOL.

Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no verified whale moves. TAO is down about 12% on the day; the HBAR price below is from an earlier report.
