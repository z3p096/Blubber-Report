Stocks drag crypto down: Bitcoin briefly touched about $73,650 overnight, its lowest since August, and sits near $74,100, down about 2% on the day. Ether is off roughly 5.6% near $2,140 and XRP about 6%. CoinGlass counts more than $1B of forced closures in a day, about nine in ten of them longs, with ether taking the biggest share. Reports tie the mood to the Nvidia probe into its CEO and to stalled US-Iran talks.

Whales keep splitting into two camps. Spot buyers are pulling ether off Binance: the two-year-dormant wallet that took about 34,600 ETH, the Aave-funded 10,000 ETH buy, and a third wallet that picked up about 8,774 ETH late last week. On Hyperliquid, the big accounts still lean short on bitcoin, with tracked shorts near $900M against under $500M of longs, and one trader banked about $2.75M by closing bitcoin and ether shorts.

Funds are still selling: US bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.28B last week. US inflation data on Tuesday, Nov 10 is the next test. Fresh searches again found no new verified whale moves for HBAR or TAO.
