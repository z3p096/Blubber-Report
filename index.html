A $21B mystery shuffle: CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost flagged more than 230,000 BTC, worth over $21 billion, moving onchain on Friday, the biggest coin-spending event of 2026 so far. Most of it had sat still for one to six months, about $4.6B of it belonged to holders of one to three years, and it looks like one entity moving coins between its own wallets rather than selling, since exchange-bound flows did not jump.

The selling pressure that is visible comes from funds and exchange deposits. Lookonchain counts another 6,998 BTC (about $572M) leaving U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs on Friday after Thursday's $635.6M exit, and one whale sent 4,000 BTC to Binance and Deribit in the morning. Bitcoin drifted from about $81,200 in the afternoon to roughly $80,600 by early evening Pacific time, down about 1% on the day, while Ether held near $2,400 as CryptoQuant data showed about 170,000 ETH leaving exchanges over two days.

Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no new large whale moves. Altcoins were weaker into the evening: NEAR and QNT fell about 6% and 5%, XRP about 2%, and HBAR, TAO and SOL each lost around 2%. Traders still mostly expect the Fed to hold rates at its Oct 28 meeting.
