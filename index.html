Big ether holders are backing off: Santiment data shows large wallets cut about 160,000 ETH, more than $348M, over the past few days, after whale holdings peaked near 26.05M ETH on Oct 26. Ether held on exchanges rose by about 100,000 ETH at the same time, which can mean more coins are ready to sell.

The Binance attacker is still the biggest whale in the room. Its roughly 18,270 BTC sit split across 19 wallets that have not moved, while on the ether side it keeps shuffling coins and borrowing on Aave. Traders are split: EmberCN tracked a four-month bull who cashed out and flipped to a 20,000 ETH short, and Lookonchain saw nine new wallets take about 1,900 BTC from Galaxy Digital.

The mood is shaky but calmer than this morning. Bitcoin hovered near $76,900 Friday afternoon, down about 1% in a day, with analysts watching $75,000 as the key floor. Binance says it froze about $3B of the stolen funds and set aside more than $6.34B to cover losses, after about $1.57B of liquidations. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
