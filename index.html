Twelve-week low: Bitcoin dropped to about $71,600 by late morning Pacific time, down roughly 3.4% on the day after touching $71,200, its weakest level in nearly 12 weeks, The Block reports. Ether is sitting near $2,000 and SOL and XRP are down about 2 to 3%. The trigger is a rough day for risk: Jensen Huang stepped down at Nvidia, Strategy is being dropped from MSCI indexes on Nov. 30, Treasury yields are near 5% and traders are betting on a December Fed hike.

Leverage is paying for it. CoinGlass counted more than $750M in liquidations over 24 hours, about $634M of them longs, and the biggest single casualty was a whale whose roughly $493M of bitcoin and ether longs were wiped out, leaving about $620,000 of $69M. Spot bitcoin ETFs have now bled for five straight days, and BlackRock moved about $444M in BTC and ETH to Coinbase Prime.

A few big wallets are leaning the other way: Wang Chun is rebuying ether near $2,054, and an Onchain Lens-tracked whale pulled 2,000 ETH off Binance and began staking. Separate searches found no fresh, verified HBAR or TAO whale moves this round; TAO fell about 4.9% and HBAR about 3%.
