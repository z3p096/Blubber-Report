A $575M bitcoin deposit leads the night: An unnamed wallet sent 7,499 BTC, about $575.1 million, to Binance early on Nov 3, and TokenPost cautions that a transfer alone does not show what the holder plans. Bitcoin has since slipped back under $77,000 to about $76,961 as traders wait on the midterm count, and ether has handed back most of a gain of more than 2%, per Bloomingbit. Coinglass figures carried by KuCoin put Hyperliquid whales at about $4.04 billion short against $3.48 billion long. The newest big bet flagged by EmberCN is a whale short about $25.43 million of Anthropic pre-market contracts and still adding.

Coins have kept leaving Binance since its hack, though Brave New Coin saw hourly deposits pass withdrawals by 03:00 UTC on Nov 3. Analyst Murphy, via Lookonchain, counts 21,064 BTC withdrawn since withdrawals began reopening on Nov 1, under 3% of its balance, and U.Today counts about 297,000 ETH gone from major exchange addresses between Oct 28 and Nov 2. The Crypto Basic ties Binance's record XRP whale outflow on Oct 29 to the theft rather than to selling, with the attacker splitting 233.46 million XRP across six wallets by Nov 2. Big corporate buyers kept going, as The Block reports Strategy added 811 BTC for about $65.5 million and Bitmine 8,573 ETH.

The mood stays cautious. Fear and Greed read 26 earlier on Nov 3, and Lookonchain shows US spot bitcoin ETFs down $398.6 million on Nov 2 and ether ETFs down $139.7 million, with BlackRock's IBIT alone losing $405.3 million. NEAR is the week's weak spot, off about 20% over seven days on KuCoin, and TAO's 24h move varies by tracker, from about −1.0% at The Defiant to −3.9% at KuCoin. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
