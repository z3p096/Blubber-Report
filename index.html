Hayes keeps selling: Bitcoin is hovering around $80,500 on Saturday evening after a morning flush took it to about $79,280, and Ether sits near $2,337, down about 2.5% on the day. CoinGlass data cited by The Block shows about $815 million of positions wiped out in 24 hours, most of them longs, with a single $11.85 million Ether long on Binance the biggest casualty. Analysts tie the slide to heavy spot Bitcoin ETF outflows on Thursday and Friday, a tech-stock sell-off, Brent crude at its highest since May, and U.S. embassy staff cuts in the Middle East.

The loudest whale is Arthur Hayes. After telling followers he had closed his family office's directional trades, Onchain Lens saw him move about 8,000 more ETH (roughly $18.7 million) and take in about $11.7 million of USDC from Galaxy Digital and Flowdesk. Early Ether holders are also cashing out, and a whale with about $214 million of Ether longs is only a few percent above its first liquidation level.

The longer view is calmer: CryptoQuant says big Bitcoin holders have added about 243,000 BTC since they stopped selling in July, and 40 new wallets have pulled about $771.5 million of BTC from Wintermute this month. NEAR (−6.0%) and HBAR (−3.5%) are among the day's weaker coins. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves. The Fed meets Oct 27–28.
