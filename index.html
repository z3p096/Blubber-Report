Calm before the Fed: Bitcoin is holding around $80,500 on Sunday morning after Saturday's slide to about $79,150, its lowest level in more than a month. Roughly $810 million of positions were wiped out in a day, most of them bullish bets. Ether sits near $2,370 and Solana is back above $100. All eyes are on the Fed meeting on Oct 27 to 28, with oil above $117 keeping inflation worries alive.

Whales are still pulling in different directions. Big Ether longs on Hyperliquid, including the $397 million position that topped up its margin overnight, are holding on, while whale positions there as a whole still lean 53% short. Bitcoin ETFs saw about $773 million leave over two sessions, even as CryptoQuant data shows large holders have been buying back coins since July.

XRP is the best performer of the coins we track (+1.6%), but on-chain data shows some big holders shrinking and coins drifting onto exchanges. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves; both are roughly flat.
