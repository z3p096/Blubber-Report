Shorts lean in as Bitcoin bounces: Bitcoin climbed back to roughly $82,500 on Sunday evening, about 1% higher on the day, and several big Hyperliquid traders used the bounce to bet against it. A wallet that Hyperbot credits with more than $126M in lifetime trading profit swapped out an older Bitcoin short and opened a bigger one, lifting its short to about $20.3M.

The bounce also stung Ether bears. An EmberCN-tracked trader who shorted about $32.7M of ETH near $2,366 lost about $6.7M of it to forced closure when Ether rose to $2,409. The rest had a wipeout line near $2,419, and Ether has since been quoted a little above that, near $2,426. Earlier in the day, the whale who held a $110M Bitcoin long gave up and closed it at a loss, then moved money into US stock tokens.

The mood is still cautious. Liquidations over the past day came to about $359M, nearly four-fifths of them longs, and US Bitcoin and Ether funds posted another week of outflows. Large Hyperliquid accounts now hold about $543M more in shorts than longs. Fresh searches found no large new HBAR or TAO whale moves; HBAR is near $0.084 and TAO is flat near $271.
