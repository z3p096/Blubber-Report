XRP whale on the move: Whale Alert caught about 82.3M XRP, roughly $106.6M, leaving a wallet that gathered its coins near $2 last winter and landing in a brand-new address. It has not reached an exchange, so it may just be a custody shuffle, but the holder is still sitting on a paper loss of about 37%. It joins this morning's 10.7-year-old Bitcoin wallet waking up with 500 BTC.

The bigger Bitcoin holders look a little less gloomy. Wallets with 1,000 to 10,000 BTC have swung from heavy selling in late September to a small 60-day gain of about 5,230 BTC, and spot ETFs took in about $527M on Monday, mostly through BlackRock. Yet the Coinbase premium is still negative, a sign U.S. buyers are holding back.

The mood is defensive. Bitcoin is near $81,700 and Ether near $2,400, both down about 1%, after Korea's Kospi fell almost 4% on AI-spending worries and Brent oil topped $111. Options traders are buying puts at twice the rate of calls, with the $80,000 put the busiest contract. NEAR is the weak spot, down almost 6%. Fresh searches found no large new HBAR or TAO whale moves.
