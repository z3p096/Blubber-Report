Bulls get flushed below $82K: Bitcoin's round trip to nearly $85,000 has turned into a slide to about $82,000, down roughly 1.6% on the day, as an Asian stock rout, Brent crude above $112 and shaky tech earnings from Tesla and Lam Research pulled risk assets lower. CoinGlass data cited by CoinDesk shows about $431M of crypto positions liquidated in 24 hours, and over the last 12 hours nearly all of it hit long bets. Ether slipped to around $2,410, and HBAR, TAO and NEAR fell about 5%.

Whales were on both sides of the flush. A single $11.8M Bitcoin long was the biggest casualty on Binance, and Lookonchain flagged another whale losing 121 BTC to liquidation. Meanwhile, the steady Bitcoin-to-Ether rotator kept going, selling another 246 BTC for about 8,300 ETH, and three big wallets are still pulling Ether off exchanges.

No large HBAR whale moves turned up in a separate search. On TAO, the only fresh sign was one Lookonchain-tracked wallet adding about 1,387 TAO, roughly $368K, inside a $7.2M altcoin shopping spree that also included QNT.
