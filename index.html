Bitcoin slips under $76,000 as votes are counted: Bloomingbit had bitcoin near $75,879 and ether near $2,234 about four hours after the first polls closed, giving back a rebound that had carried bitcoin to about $77,828 earlier on Tuesday, per The Motley Fool. Bloomingbit blames worry that Democrats could win both chambers and slow pro-crypto policy. CryptoTicker's last update had no House or Senate call yet, and Polymarket pricing a Democratic sweep at 85.5%. On-chain, the newest flag is Lookonchain catching the Binance hacker still turning SOL into BTC through several hops, with no amounts disclosed. The largest single move remains an unnamed wallet's 7,499 BTC deposit to Binance, about $575.1 million.

Binance flows are starting to turn. CoinDesk, using data the exchange shared, counts $10.97 billion withdrawn against $5.69 billion deposited in the first 46 hours after withdrawals reopened, and Bitcoin.com says DefiLlama later showed about $3.25 billion of net inflows over 24 hours. Some big holders are still pulling coins off exchanges: Onchain Lens saw a whale take about 303.86 BTC, about $23.4 million, from Binance and spread it across wallets, and Lookonchain saw a three-month-old wallet take 5,887 ETH and 69.125 million PONS, about $27.42 million in all, from OKX in 12 hours. Coinglass figures carried by KuCoin earlier put Hyperliquid whales at about $4.04 billion short against $3.48 billion long.

The mood is cautious but off its lows, with KuCoin's report showing Fear and Greed at 34, up from 26. Funds are still leaving: Lookonchain's Nov 3 update shows bitcoin ETFs down 5,942 BTC, about $459 million, and ether ETFs down 86,589 ETH, about $197 million, in one day. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
