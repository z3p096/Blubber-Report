Back above $81K: Bitcoin is near $81,100, up less than 1% on the day, after US data showed third-quarter growth of 3.4%, ahead of forecasts, while September PCE inflation landed as expected at 3.6% a year. BTC briefly touched about $81,500 on Bitstamp even as tech stocks opened lower. Ether is holding around $2,344.

The bounce hurt bears more than bulls: Coinglass counted about $156M in short liquidations over the past day against about $103M in longs. Santiment also tracked roughly 14,942 BTC leaving exchanges, the biggest outflow in five weeks. On Hyperliquid, one address is sitting on a fresh 19,491 ETH long worth about $45M.

The selling hasn't vanished. The wallet that dumped about 1.02M SOL is still the largest seller on the board, and an early Ether ICO wallet sent coins to Coinbase. TAO's fresh buyer is still KR1 with 5,000 coins. NEAR is the weakest coin here, down about 5%, and a fresh HBAR search found no new large whale moves.
