Bleeding slows, fear stays: Bitcoin is back near $69,400 Friday morning, still down about 4% on the day after touching its lowest level since mid-August. Ether has steadied near $1,965 and XRP and SOL are roughly flat. About $1.47B of leveraged bets were wiped out in a day, mostly longs, while a hot October inflation reading pushed odds of a December Fed hike to about 90%. The Fear and Greed index is stuck at 20, extreme fear.

The whale picture is two-sided. Leveraged longs keep getting flushed, with Lookonchain tracking another trader whose $140M-plus long book was fully liquidated, and Wintermute still holds about $91M of Hyperliquid shorts. But some deep-pocketed buyers are pulling coins off Binance: Onchain Lens saw one wallet withdraw about 1,042 BTC in 19 hours, and another ETH whale has taken roughly 28,430 ETH off the exchange over three months.

Separate HBAR and TAO searches again turned up no fresh, verified whale moves. HBAR is about flat near $0.064 and TAO is down about 2% near $212.
