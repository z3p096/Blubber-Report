Back under $80K: Bitcoin slipped below $80,000 again on Saturday and trades near $79,500, down about 2.4% on the day, while Ether sits just under $2,300, off about 4.4%. CoinGlass counted roughly $837 million of liquidations in 24 hours, about 83% of them longs. Onchain Lens says two Hyperliquid whales lost $8.05 million of Bitcoin longs in the dip, and the Lookonchain-tracked whale with about $214 million of Ether longs now has its first liquidation level only a couple of percent below the price.

Big-wallet signals still pull both ways. Forty new wallets have taken about $771.5 million of BTC out of Wintermute this month, but about $2.6 billion of BTC landed on exchanges in a single day, one whale sent about 950 BTC to Binance and Deribit on Friday, and Arthur Hayes has cashed out of his directional bets.

NEAR (−8.1%), SOL (−5.2%) and TAO (−5.1%) are among the weakest coins. Bitcoin has closed below its 50-day average for the first time since August after fresh Iran tension, higher oil and a $635 million day of spot ETF outflows, with the Fed decision on Oct 28 and PCE data on Oct 29 next. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
