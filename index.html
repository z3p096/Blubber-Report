Binance refills the vault: Binance says it has put $6.34B of company money into its user fund pool and now fully covers ten assets hit by the breach, including BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP, with withdrawals set to restart on Nov 1. Whale Alert then saw 7,499 BTC, about $581M, leave Binance's SAFU reserve for an unlabeled wallet on Saturday morning, a transfer the exchange has not explained.

Not every big holder is calm. Nasdaq-listed Next Technology Holding says its board cleared it to sell or borrow against roughly 5,833 BTC, about $449M, citing the hack and quantum risk, and about 550,000 SOL went to Coinbase Institutional. On the other side, ether whales kept pulling coins off FalconX and OKX, and a Hyperliquid ETH short is sitting on close to $8M of paper profit.

Prices are steady after Friday's $643M bitcoin ETF exit. Bitcoin sits near $77,300 and ether near $2,240, while NEAR and QNT gain about 3%. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
