Binance wallets drained: Starting around 1:24 p.m. PT, a flood of ETH, USDT, USDC and dozens of other tokens left Binance hot wallets for brand-new addresses whose funding traces back to Tornado Cash. Early tallies run from about $3.3B on Ethereum to at least $5.57B across Ethereum, Bitcoin and Tron, including roughly 18,270 BTC. Binance says it is investigating; founder CZ said it does look like a hack but that user funds are safe, and credited Tether and Circle with freezing about $971M and $823M.

The market flinched. Bitcoin dropped to about $79,100, down 2.7% on the day, Ether fell about 3.9% near $2,270 and BNB lost more than 5%, with roughly $629M of positions, almost all longs, wiped out in an hour. Crypto was already soft after weak tech earnings dragged the Nasdaq lower. NEAR is the hardest hit here, down about 12%.

Before the shock, big Bitcoin buyers were busy: TokenPost counted 14,942 BTC leaving exchanges, the biggest outflow in five weeks, with one buyer taking about 11,326 BTC worth roughly $903M. Ether whales were still trimming. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves; KR1's 5,000 TAO buy is still the latest.
