Fear at the floor: Bitcoin is trading in the $64,000 to $65,500 range after Sunday's drop to about $61,950, roughly 14% lower on the week. CoinGlass data cited by Bitcoin.com shows about $1.7B of liquidations in 24 hours, four-fifths of them longs, and the Fear and Greed index is stuck at 10. Ether sits near $1,836, XRP near $0.97 and SOL near $73, all down on the day.

Flood notes that almost all of the weekend bitcoin selling ran through Coinbase, a sign of US-based sellers.

Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new verified whale moves. TAO is down about 10% on the day near $187, while HBAR is up about 1.4%. NEAR and QNT carry over from earlier updates; the live feed replaces them in your browser.
