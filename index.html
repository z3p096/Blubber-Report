The clearest whale damage was forced rather than chosen: one trader lost a $33.7M BTC long to liquidation, then a $2.57M TRUMP short hours later. Others leaned in, with a new wallet pulling 10,000 ETH from Coinbase. HBAR searches again found no fresh large wallet moves, and in TAO the only real move found is a subnet team's 8,450 TAO burn. Next up: about $2.7B of bitcoin options expire around midnight PT, then the US jobs report lands at 5:30 a.m. PT Friday, with economists looking for about 65,000 new jobs.
