Selling pressure holds: Crypto stayed under water into Thursday afternoon. Bitcoin is around $81,400, down about 4% on the day after nearly touching $85,000 on Wednesday, and Ether is near $2,390. Smaller coins are falling harder: QNT is off about 10%, NEAR more than 8%, and HBAR and TAO over 6%. A roughly 2% Nasdaq drop on AI-spending worries, Brent oil near $112 and nervousness before next week's Fed meeting are keeping traders cautious, and about $431M of leveraged bets were wiped out in a day, mostly longs.

Whales are hedging rather than fleeing. Hyperliquid's biggest accounts now hold about $572M more in shorts than longs, and Lookonchain flagged a trader forced out of a 121 BTC long worth about $9.9M on the way down. At the same time, U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have taken in about $1.08B over three sessions, and the BTC-to-ETH rotation whale kept swapping into Ether.

Separate searches for HBAR and TAO again turned up no new large whale moves this afternoon. The only recent TAO trace is still one Lookonchain-tracked wallet adding about 1,387 TAO in a $7.2M altcoin basket.
