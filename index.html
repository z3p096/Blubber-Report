Selling is back as bitcoin slips to $77,000: Bitcoin gave up Sunday night's high near $78,750 and sat just under $77,000 by midday Monday Eastern time, about 1.4% lower on the day. Over $100 million in long bets were wiped out in a single hour, and Bitcoin.com counts $256 million of liquidations across crypto, more than $176 million of it longs. Ether slid to about $2,250.

Big wallets stayed busy around exchanges. TokenPost's three-hour tally shows about $412 million in USDT going from unknown wallets to Bitfinex and Binance, while about $104 million in bitcoin left Coinbase Institutional. Strategy added 811 BTC for about $65.5 million. Hacker money is still part of the picture too: Lookonchain says the Binance attackers swapped 486,970 SOL into bitcoin, and the wallet behind the weekend's 27,553 ETH purchase is suspected of links to a theft, though that is not confirmed. Coinglass last showed Hyperliquid whales leaning short, $4.15 billion against $3.59 billion in longs.

The mood is wary before Tuesday's US midterms. Last week's spot funds lost about $432 million in ether, $203 million in bitcoin and $71 million in Solana. Binance's reserve proof showed at least 100% backing for 10 tokens as of Oct 31, with more withdrawals due Tuesday and another snapshot Wednesday. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
