Sleeping Bitcoin wakes up: Two wallets that had not moved in about three years sent a combined 2,571 BTC, roughly $206 million, to Coinbase Prime, according to Lookonchain. Coins landing on an institutional desk like that are often lined up for a sale, though nothing has been sold yet as far as the data shows.

Ether holders are also heading for the exits. Two large wallets sent about 5,070 ETH (around $11.7 million) to Kraken and Binance, one of them at a loss, while the 2015 ICO whale keeps unloading. Over the weekend Arthur Hayes said his fund Maelstrom sold its whole directional book, including 6,400 ETH, citing macro and geopolitical risk.

The mood is cautious ahead of Wednesday's Fed decision. Bitcoin slipped just under $80,000 and Ether sits near $2,324, while XRP is the standout with a gain of about 1.5% and QNT is up about 3.6%. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
