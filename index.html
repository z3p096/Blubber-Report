Sunday's unlocks land quietly: Ripple's monthly escrow release started on schedule, with Whale Alert logging 500M XRP, about $626M, coming out of escrow just after midnight UTC. In past months Ripple has relocked most of each release, so the unlock is not by itself a sign of selling.

Elsewhere the evening was about coins drifting toward exchanges: an unlabeled wallet sent 800 BTC, roughly $62M, to Kraken, while the big SOL deposits to Coinbase Prime and Coinbase Institutional from earlier Saturday still stand out. Binance is due to reopen withdrawals on Nov 1 after covering about 90% of the stolen assets.

The mood stays cautious but calm. Bitcoin sits near $77,700 and ether near $2,270, Coinglass's fear and greed gauge reads 31, in "fear," and QNT and NEAR again lead with gains of about 4% and 3%. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches turned up no new large whale moves.
