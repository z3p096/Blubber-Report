Shorts keep getting squeezed as bitcoin holds above $80,000: Bitcoin is near $80,360 on Kraken, up about 3.4% in a day from an election-night low near $75,600, and ether leads at about $2,422, up 5.7%. CoinGlass figures in CryptoSlate show about $913M of liquidations in 24 hours, with shorts taking most of the worst hour. A Nov. 4 TokenPost tally had Hyperliquid whale shorts at about $4.10 billion against $3.62 billion of longs, and Lookonchain saw one address liquidated six times in two hours, losing about $15.45 million of its short.

Coins keep churning around big desks with no clear story. Whale Alert logged 1,668 BTC entering Coinbase's institutional desk and 1,131 BTC leaving during the rally, and earlier 1,086 BTC in and 668 BTC out. Onchain Lens saw a whale pull about 303.86 BTC, about $23.4M, off Binance and split it across wallets. Arkham data shows the Binance hackers still hold about 182,443 SOL, and The Crypto Basic has Binance's XRP whale-versus-retail outflow spread at 98.4%, though it warns the hack skews that reading.

Caution remains. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.16 billion over three sessions, and ether ETFs have seven straight outflow days, with buyers averaging just under $3,200 by Bloomberg Intelligence's estimate. CryptoQuant data in CryptoSlate shows past post-midterm drawdowns of 18.5% to 49.7%. The Fear and Greed Index only rose from 26 to 34, and October jobs data lands Nov. 6. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is still Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
