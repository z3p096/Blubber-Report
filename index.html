Big wallets wait, small ones sell: CryptoQuant data still shows retail, not whales, driving Bitcoin deposits to Binance. Small holders sent about $10.75 billion over 30 days while whale deposits shrank to about $3.8 billion. XRP shows a softer version of the same picture: Santiment counts fewer wallets holding 10 million to 100 million XRP, and about 47 million more XRP now sits on exchanges.

The whales who are active are mostly using leverage. Two separate traders hold large leveraged Ether longs, one about $214 million and another, per EmberCN, about $396 million across ETH and BTC, and both have added margin to push their liquidation prices down to roughly $2,180.

Prices barely moved into the evening. Bitcoin sits just under $80,000 and Ether near $2,314 as traders cut risk before Wednesday's Fed decision, while QNT is up about 18% on the day. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again turned up no new large whale moves.
