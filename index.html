Bitcoin bounces, but big wallets stay short: Bitcoin is back near $77,828 after an intraday low of $76,268, yet Coinglass figures carried by KuCoin put Hyperliquid whales at about $4.04 billion short against $3.48 billion long. The standout is address 0x5b5d..60, on a 5x ether short from $2,322.72 and about $5.98 million ahead on paper. A few bet the other way: one unnamed wallet is long 35.03 million XRP, about $44 million. Others have left crypto for oil, with a whale tracked by analyst Yu Jin selling ether and now holding about $148 million of crude, about $540,000 under water.

The exchange drain after the Binance hack is the other story. U.Today counts about 297,000 ETH, or 5.4%, gone from major exchange addresses between Oct 28 and Nov 2. Binance logged $5.28 billion of net outflows over 46 hours, though hourly deposits had moved ahead of withdrawals by 03:00 UTC on Nov 3, and CryptoQuant contributor Gaah sees no bank run. Whale Alert's two 40 million XRP transfers, about $101 million, look like Binance clearing a compromised address into new internal wallets, per U.Today. Old coins are stirring too, with a wallet idle for over six years moving 999.953 BTC. CryptoQuant's Darkfost adds that small deposits to Binance now average just 281 BTC a day, a sign retail has gone quiet. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $398.6 million on Nov 2 and ether ETFs $139.8 million.

The mood is cautious on US midterm election day. Fear and Greed read 26 earlier today, and Bitcoin.com counts more than $365 million liquidated in a day, $286 million of it longs. CoinDesk says bitcoin is on course for a fifth weekly loss, though Motley Fool data shows XRP up about 2.7% and leading our eight. NEAR looks weakest at about −6.6% on CoinGecko, though price trackers disagree widely on its move. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
