Big bitcoin moves, mostly Binance's own: As Binance reopens withdrawals one network at a time (Arbitrum and TRX joined early Sunday), Whale Alert flagged 3,000 BTC, about $234M, leaving the exchange and 1,600 BTC, about $125M, arriving. Co-founder He Yi says most of these big transfers are Binance shifting coins from cold storage to new hot wallets, and that Saturday night's 2,000 BTC outflow went to a fresh Binance address.

The whale worth watching is the attacker. Lookonchain and Arkham show the wallet holding about 970,500 stolen SOL swapping it into bitcoin through Relay, with roughly 487,000 SOL moved within an hour. The Symbiosis bridge says it caught hack-linked funds trying to pass through its protocol and has contacted Binance.

Prices barely flinched: bitcoin sits near $78,000, up under 1% after its tightest daily range since Oct 17, ether is near $2,270, and the fear and greed index reads 35, still fearful. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
