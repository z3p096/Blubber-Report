BlackRock moves, leverage bleeds: Bitcoin is trading around $68,300 on Friday afternoon, down about 4% on the day. Roughly $1.6B of leveraged bets were wiped out in 24 hours, nearly all of them longs, and spot bitcoin ETFs have now posted six straight days of outflows. A hot producer-price report has a December Fed hike fully priced in, and the Fear and Greed index sits at 20.

The biggest new whale move came from BlackRock, whose ETF wallet shifted about $206M of bitcoin and ether to Coinbase Prime, a route often used ahead of ETF redemptions. Elsewhere the pain shows in the leverage crowd: the well-known "First Set 10 Big Goals" trader has given back more than $93M of gains since October and says he has closed all leverage, while Wintermute keeps adding shorts. A few buyers are still pulling BTC off Binance.

HBAR fell about 4% to near $0.063. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no fresh, verified whale moves; TAO, NEAR and QNT prices are carried from the last update.
