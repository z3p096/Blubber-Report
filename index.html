Coins in, coins out: The biggest Bitcoin move of the day is still the 11,330 BTC, about $903M, that a five-month-quiet whale pulled off Binance, a sign of long-term holding. Since then, Onchain Lens saw BlackRock's IBIT fund wallet send 1,698 BTC, about $136M, to Coinbase Prime, and a Japanese whale idle for nearly two years take another 65 BTC off local exchanges.

Selling pressure has not gone away. CryptoQuant says recent buyers keep sending coins to exchanges at a loss, two wallets idle for three years moved 2,571 BTC to Coinbase Prime, and a long-time Ether holder just sent its last 3,084 ETH to Binance. Big XRP wallets, meanwhile, hold their largest share of supply since 2022, per Santiment.

Traders are waiting on Wednesday's Fed decision. Bitcoin is hovering right around $80,000, which CoinDesk's sources call key support, with Ether near $2,345 and XRP at $1.32 after Ripple won approval for its U.S. trust bank. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no fresh large whale moves today.
