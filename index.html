Binance pledges to pay: Binance has halted deposits and withdrawals and says it will cover all losses from the suspected hack that began around 1:20 p.m. PT. It has not given a final loss figure; outside estimates range from about $5.3B (Glassnode, Coinglass) to roughly $5.8B, including about 17,985 BTC and one sweep of roughly 167,000 ETH. Binance says about $2.54B has now been frozen, mostly by Tether and Circle, with Ripple and others adding smaller amounts.

The attacker is already cashing out. Trackers say the hacker swapped its entire 7.77 trillion PEPE stash for about 14,049 ETH, knocking PEPE down about 12%, and still holds tens of millions of dollars in ENA, LINK, AAVE and UNI that could hit the market next. Whale Alert also flagged 185M XRP, about $236M, leaving Binance for a fresh wallet; it is not known whether that is related.

The mood is fearful. Bitcoin sits near $78,500, down 3.5% on the day, Ether is down about 4.4% and BNB nearly 8%, with roughly $1.4B in leveraged bets wiped out in 24 hours. TAO, NEAR and QNT are the hardest hit here, each down 11% to 13%. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
