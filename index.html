Flat Sunday: Bitcoin is stuck near $80,200 this afternoon, barely changed on the day after Saturday's dip to about $79,150, its lowest in over a month. Ether is around $2,330, briefly poking back above $2,350 on Binance late in the day. Traders are waiting on the Fed meeting on Oct 27 to 28, with Brent crude above $117 keeping inflation fears alive, and US spot Bitcoin funds lost roughly $770 million over Oct 22 and 23.

Whales are still split. A Hyperliquid address holds an $89 million Bitcoin short, and whale positions there lean slightly short overall, while a $397 million Ether and Bitcoin long is hanging on after adding margin. Small leveraged Ether longs are getting squeezed: well-known trader Machi Big Brother was wiped out on a 25x long this morning and jumped straight back in with a smaller one.

HBAR is down about 13% on the week, among the worst large coins, while TAO and NEAR are ticking up. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again found no new large whale moves.
