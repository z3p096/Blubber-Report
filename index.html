Whales head for the exits: About 32,380 BTC, worth roughly $2.6 billion, flowed onto exchanges in a single day, the biggest one-day inflow since April, according to data shared by Cointelegraph. Coins sent to exchanges are not always sold, but inflows this large usually mean more supply is ready to hit the market.

BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes added to the cautious mood. Lookonchain tracked him selling about $22.3 million of crypto, including 6,400 ETH, and he said he closed his fund's directional bets over macro and geopolitical worries. Friday's 230,000 BTC shuffle, which CryptoQuant reads as one entity moving its own wallets, is still the week's largest move.

Bitcoin sat near $80,600 on Saturday morning (UTC), down about 1.3% on the day, with Ether near $2,370. Altcoins fell harder: NEAR lost about 8.5%, TAO about 4%, and XRP more than 3%. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves. Traders are watching the Fed's Oct 28 meeting.
