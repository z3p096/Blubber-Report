Through the floor: Bitcoin broke below $70,000 tonight and briefly touched about $69,000, its lowest level since mid-August, and was trading near $68,900 a little later, down about 6.5% on the day. Ether is back under $2,000 at roughly $1,950, while XRP, SOL and TAO are each down roughly 4% to 5.5% and QNT about 9%. A hotter-than-expected October inflation reading has traders pricing in a December Fed hike, the dollar index is at a 19-month high, and the Fear and Greed index sits at 19, “extreme fear.”

Leverage is getting flushed again. CoinGlass counts more than $1.3B in futures liquidations over 24 hours, and EmberCN says two whales lost $60M of bitcoin longs as the price hit $70,000, one of them the same address that was liquidated six times earlier today. Long-held coins keep heading to exchanges, and spot bitcoin ETFs have now lost about $1.58B in November.

A few big wallets are still buying the dip, including the “7 Siblings” ether whale and Wang Chun. Separate searches again found no fresh, verified HBAR or TAO whale moves.
