Sellers under pressure: The final Farside tally for Friday shows U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $643M, their biggest one-day exit since June, and ether ETFs shed another $156M. Big wallets are not all heading for the exit. The mood stays cautious. Bitcoin sits near $77,200 and ether near $2,240, with bitcoin on track for a second losing October as tech stocks and gold fell on Friday. The coins taken in the Binance breach remain a cloud over the market. NEAR is up about 8% and QNT about 3%. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
