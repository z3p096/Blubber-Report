A billion-dollar bet on lower prices: Two of Hyperliquid's top wallets now hold about $1.06 billion in short positions between them, according to Bitcoin.com's reading of Hypurrscan data. The larger one, 0x5b5d, is short about $617.5 million across 22 markets, led by ether and bitcoin, and it also leads the exchange's 30-day profit leaderboard.

Others are moving the opposite way, or at least moving. The whale Lookonchain tracked buying 27,553 ETH for about $63 million has not been identified, and a wallet quiet for more than six years just shifted its full 999.953 BTC, about $78 million. That is a transfer, not a confirmed sale. Whale Alert also logged a second 3,000 BTC withdrawal from Binance to an unknown wallet.

The mood stays cautious before Tuesday's US midterms. Bitcoin sits near $78,000 and ether near $2,260, with the Fear and Greed gauge at 37. Binance now says its full proof of reserves is due Tuesday UTC, after missing Monday, while DefiLlama shows its tracked assets down about $16.5 billion since Oct 29, a figure that also reflects the hack and lower prices. NEAR and TAO are the weak spots, down about 6% and 3%. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
