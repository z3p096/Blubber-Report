Oil crushes the rebound: Bitcoin opened Thursday near $84,000, its best level in weeks, then gave it all back as Brent crude stayed above $111 a barrel and Asian stocks slid. It now sits around $81,600, down about 2% on the day, with Ether just under $2,400. Bitcoin's share of the crypto market is near 60%, its highest since May, as traders hide in BTC while smaller coins like HBAR, TAO and NEAR drop 4% to 5%.

Whales were caught on both sides. CoinGlass counted about $431M of liquidations in 24 hours, led by an $11.8M Bitcoin long, and Lookonchain flagged a 121 BTC whale wiped out. The steady Bitcoin-to-Ether rotator keeps buying ETH, while big XRP holders are sending more coins to Binance than at any time since March, a pattern that often comes before selling.

Separate searches turned up no fresh large HBAR whale moves. For TAO, the only recent sign is one Lookonchain-tracked wallet adding about 1,387 TAO in a $7.2M altcoin buying spree.
