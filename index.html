Sunday-night flush: Just before the weekly close, ether dropped about 7% in 15 minutes to near $2,060 and bitcoin slid to about $73,510, wiping out roughly $745M of positions in one hour, almost all of them bets on higher prices. Ether finished the day near $2,122, down about 5%, and its price against bitcoin hit the lowest level since September.

Whales were caught on both sides. EmberCN flagged one wallet that lost about $140M of bitcoin and ether longs to liquidation and is now about $61M in the red, while another address lost a $54M ether long and well-known trader Machi Big Brother's Hyperliquid account was emptied. The big short sellers, led by Abraxas Capital's roughly $452M book, came out ahead.

The fear reading sits at 28, bitcoin is set for a fifth straight losing week, and US inflation data on Tuesday, Nov 10 is the next test. Searches turned up no new verified whale moves for HBAR or TAO.
