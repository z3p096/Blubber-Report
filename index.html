Institutions on the move: Early Saturday the biggest flows came from Wall Street wallets. Onchain Lens spotted BlackRock's ether ETF wallets sending about 37,470 ETH, roughly $83M, to Coinbase Prime. That came a day after U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted about $409M of net outflows in their first session since the Binance breach, by Farside's early count.

Dip buyers are still around. An EmberCN-tracked wallet now holds about 9,376 ETH after a $17.2M purchase, Lookonchain flagged 9,625 ETH pulled from FalconX, and a widely followed trader says he rebuilt his full bitcoin long between $76,000 and $78,000 after selling two-thirds near $76,000. Hyperliquid whales still lean short overall.

The mood is cautious. Bitcoin hovers near $77,200 and ether near $2,240, with the whole crypto market worth about $2.69T. The 18,270 BTC taken from Binance still sits unspent across 19 wallets, and Binance has promised proof-of-reserves data by Monday. NEAR bounced about 8% and TAO held near $236. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches turned up no new large whale moves.
