Underwater sellers: Recent Bitcoin buyers are now sending coins to exchanges mostly at a loss. CryptoQuant analyst Axel Adler Jr. says loss-making deposits beat profitable ones by about 18,600 BTC over the past day, and profitable deposits made up just 16% of the week, down from 75%. On top of that, Lookonchain says miner MARA sold 1,000 BTC (about $80M), and two wallets idle for three years sent 2,571 BTC to Coinbase Prime.

Not everyone is heading for the door. Strive bought about 6,182 BTC (roughly $511M) in the week to Oct. 23, and a wallet tied to the UXLINK hack is holding a $70M leveraged Ether long. Ether whales, though, keep moving coins onto Kraken and Binance.

The mood is nervous before Wednesday's Fed decision. Bitcoin is just under $80,000 and Ether is near $2,313, while XRP is up about 2% and QNT about 6%. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again turned up no fresh large whale moves.
