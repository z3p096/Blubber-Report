Top traders pile into shorts: TokenPost's Satoshi Index, built on Satoshi Note data, shows the 100 best 24-hour performers on Hyperliquid, Lighter, dYdX and Pacifica holding about $2.56 billion short against about $180 million long, so only about 7% long, down from about 20% a day earlier. That group is reselected hourly, so not every dollar is a new bet. One Hyperliquid address, 0xfc2713...9d9d, alone holds a bitcoin short of about $116 million. Not everyone agrees: another address cut about $23.42 million from its ether short and flipped to a bitcoin long, while trader 0xf081 took a loss of about $6 million on longs and then opened about $43 million of new ether and HYPE longs.

The other big flow is money leaving Binance after its Oct 29 hack. On-chain data shared by CEO Richard Teng shows $5.28 billion in net outflows over 46 hours, though by 03:00 UTC on Nov 3 hourly deposits were ahead of withdrawals by about $10 million. TokenPost counts $6.1 billion out on Nov 1 and $2.57 billion on Nov 2, so counts differ but the pace is slowing. The hacker has split 233.46 million stolen XRP, about $293 million, across six new wallets, and about 2 million SOL left Binance for an unidentified wallet on Nov 1. Funds are shedding too: US spot bitcoin ETFs lost $398.6 million on Nov 2, led by $405.3 million out of BlackRock's IBIT, and ether ETFs lost $139.8 million.

The mood is fearful on US midterm election day, with the Fear and Greed index at 26, down from 37, and a chip stock selloff weighing on risk. Even so, bitcoin has bounced to about $77,485 from a 24-hour low near $76,389, back above the $76,800 line Wintermute flagged. XRP leads our eight, up about 2.4%, and NEAR is weakest, down about 7% as voters approved a cut to its token issuance. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no large wallet moves; the nearest HBAR signal is Binance top-trader accounts sitting about 60.8% long.
