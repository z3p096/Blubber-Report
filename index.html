A tech rout wipes out bitcoin's midweek bounce: Bitcoin ran from about $81,100 to a low near $75,470 after the US open, as chip stocks led the Nasdaq down as much as about 5%. It now sits near $76,450, down 5.2% in a day, with ether near $2,266, down 7.1%. CoinGlass counts about $1.03B of liquidations in 24 hours, roughly 89% of it longs, the reverse of this morning's short squeeze.

Coins were already heading toward trading venues before the drop: Trump Media's roughly 4,259 BTC to Crypto.com, MARA's 1,650 BTC to trading platforms, and 28,690 BTC from short-term holders to exchanges in a day. CryptoQuant's Binance whale ratio also climbed near 1.0. None of that proves selling or caused the slide, and no large whale sale tied to the drop has been reported yet. Coinglass had Hyperliquid whales about $4.25B short against $3.77B long before it hit.

The mood turned quickly: the VIX jumped to 28 and Lookonchain's 7-day ETF tally shows about $1.12B out of bitcoin funds and $423M out of ether funds, despite small inflows on the latest day. XRP stays mixed, with Binance reserves up 280M coins while million-coin wallets hit a 2026 high. HBAR searches again found no large wallet moves, and in TAO the only real move found is a subnet team's 8,450 TAO buyback and burn. The US jobs report lands Friday.
