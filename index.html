Bitcoin punches through $80,000 and the shorts pay for it: After a midterm-night low near $75,600, bitcoin climbed past $80,600 and ether reached about $2,435. Crypto.news, citing CoinGlass, counted about $378 million of shorts wiped out in a single hour. BigGo credits the Democratic sweep, a softer dollar and short covering. Earlier today Hyperliquid whales still leaned short, about $4.10 billion against $3.62 billion long per Coinglass, which left big short bets exposed.

Large wallets are moving both ways at Coinbase's institutional desk. Whale Alert logged 1,131 BTC leaving for a new wallet within the same hour that 855 BTC and 813 BTC came in, along with 21,294 ETH. None has a named owner. Nexo says election-day bitcoin options blocks hit about $660 million, with call buying far ahead of call selling, and Coinglass shows ether open interest up 7.27% in a day to about $30.46 billion. Separately, The Crypto Basic has Binance's XRP whale-versus-retail outflow spread at 98.4%, its highest since October 2024, though it warns the hack skews that reading.

Not everyone is convinced: US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.16 billion over three sessions, and Bitcoin.com has ether ETFs on a seven-session losing streak. CryptoQuant's Darkfost said earlier that selling looks exhausted but bitcoin's upside needs fresh demand. Onchain Lens says Binance-hack wallets have begun mixing about $800,000 of SOL. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
