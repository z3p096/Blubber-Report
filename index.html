Big shorts get squeezed as bitcoin sits near $80,000: Bitcoin has climbed from an election-night low near $75,600 and ether is leading at about $2,407, up roughly 5% in a day. CoinGape puts the whole market up 3.38% at $2.69 trillion, pointing to the Democratic midterm wins and cheaper oil. Hyperliquid whales were on the wrong foot: a Nov. 4 tally in TokenPost had shorts at about $4.10 billion against $3.62 billion of longs. Lookonchain saw one address liquidated six times in two hours, losing about 199.83 BTC, or $15.45 million, of its short.

Coins keep moving around large desks with no clear story. Whale Alert logged 1,131 BTC leaving Coinbase's institutional desk within the hour that 1,668 BTC came in, and Onchain Lens flagged 1,073.49 BTC going to a new wallet. Arkham data shows the Binance hackers still hold about 182,443 SOL after sending most of the stolen coins toward bitcoin through THORChain. The Crypto Basic has Binance's XRP whale-versus-retail outflow spread at 98.4%, though it warns the hack skews that reading.

Caution remains. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.16 billion over three sessions, though the daily outflow slowed to $115.6 million on Nov. 3, and Bitcoin.com has ether ETFs on a seven-session losing streak. The Fear and Greed Index only rose from 26 to 34, and October jobs data lands Nov. 6. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; the nearest signal is still Binance top-trader accounts leaning about 60.8% long on HBAR and 64.8% long on TAO, which Blockchain.News says is no read on broader conviction.
