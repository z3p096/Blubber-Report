Bitcoin is sitting on Wintermute's line: Onchain Lens reports the market maker's Hyperliquid address is short about $101.33 million, with about $3.35 million in paper profit, and that the firm warned a break below $76,800 could bring more downside. Bitcoin is near $76,870 as this update goes out. Another Hyperliquid trader, 0xecb63c…2b00, went the other way overnight, cutting an ether short and going long bitcoin.

Coins keep leaving Binance. Whale Alert flagged 50 million XRP, worth over $62.4 million, going to an unknown holder, while CZ says the exchange covered its $6.3 billion hack from its own balance sheet, with about 95% of losses replaced and about $3 billion frozen on-chain. One big "whale buy" also looks different now: Bitquery ties the 27,553 ETH purchase to suspected stolen funds. CryptoQuant's Darkfost says large XRP deposits to Binance hit a seven-month high in October, though he notes a deposit is not proof of a sale. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.

The mood has soured on US midterm election day. CoinGabbar, citing Alternative.me, has the Fear and Greed index at 26, down from 37 a day earlier. Lookonchain's latest one-day tally shows about $614 million leaving bitcoin funds and $102 million leaving ether funds. NEAR is still the weakest of our eight, down about 9.8%, with QNT next at about 5.5%.
