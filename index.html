Old Ether whales cash out as a new one buys: An investor who got 200,000 ETH in Ethereum's 2015 sale sent its last 7,594 ETH (~$17.8M) to Kraken, finishing a years-long exit, EmberCN reports. A longtime Lido staker also moved 3,084 ETH to Binance, and Whale Alert saw 30,375 ETH (~$70.8M) go to Coinbase Institutional. On the other side, Onchain Lens says one buyer took about 14,600 ETH (~$34M) plus LINK, AAVE and UNI from BitGo within an hour.

In Bitcoin, the big picture is still coins leaving exchanges: CryptoQuant counted about $1B in net outflows on Tuesday, led by one wallet pulling 11,330 BTC off Binance. Leveraged traders lean the other way. Coinglass data shows the biggest Hyperliquid accounts holding about $4.55B in shorts against $3.97B in longs.

Prices edged higher into the Fed's rate decision on Wednesday, with Bitcoin near $80,800, Ether near $2,345 and XRP up almost 3%. Markets mostly expect no rate change. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again found no fresh large whale moves.
