Money is walking out of Binance: Since withdrawals restarted, DefiLlama data shows about $2.5 billion more leaving Binance than arriving, a bit under 2% of the roughly $134 billion it holds, while OKX and Bybit took in small net inflows. Binance now says it has refilled about 95% of the stolen assets from its own funds, up from 90% a day earlier, with a full proof of reserves due by Nov 3.

The attacker is still the biggest whale on the board. Elliptic estimates the hackers hold about $3.3 billion, including 18,270 BTC, and move funds every hour through a Tron wallet toward THORChain. Lookonchain shows about half of a 970,500 SOL stash already swapped toward bitcoin, and one clumsy sale of roughly $5M in PROM tokens returned about $71.

Prices stay calm: bitcoin is near $77,700, up 0.3%, with trading volume down by almost half and coins on balance flowing off exchanges. Ether holds near $2,270. A Hyperliquid trader doubled an ether short to about $48.7M. Fresh HBAR and TAO searches found no new large whale moves.
