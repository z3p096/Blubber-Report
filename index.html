Fed-shock hangover: Bitcoin has climbed back to about $65,200 after an overnight drop to roughly $62,000, leaving it down about 2% on the day. The slide came as traders priced in close to 90% odds of a December Fed rate hike after hot inflation and jobs numbers, while US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.07B last week. Ether is near $1,820, SOL is around $72, and the Fear and Greed index sits at 10.

Big wallets are split. Lookonchain says wallets linked to Erik Voorhees bought about 63,500 ETH (roughly $125M) on the dip and 7 Siblings added another 31,500 ETH, and CryptoQuant shows Binance's ETH reserve at its lowest since 2021. On Hyperliquid, one address is holding a 1,686 BTC long worth about $109M, but leverage keeps biting: a HYPE long whale is pouring in margin to avoid liquidation after a record $60M of HYPE longs were wiped out in a day.

Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no verified whale moves; TAO is down about 12% in 24 hours. NEAR and QNT prices below carry over from earlier updates; the live feed replaces them in your browser.
