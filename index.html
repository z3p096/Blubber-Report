Trump Media moves about $342M of bitcoin as the rebound holds: Arkham data in CoinDesk shows about 4,259 BTC going to Crypto.com in two transfers at 02:17 UTC, from wallets labeled as collateral for the company's 2028 convertible notes. That alone does not prove a sale, but a Nov 2 filing says noteholders can ask for repayment on Nov 30 and that bitcoin may have to be sold if refinancing falls short. Miner MARA also sent 1,650 BTC, about $132.8M, to trading platforms within 12 hours. Bitcoin sits near $80,485, up 4.7% in a day, and ether near $2,419, up 6.9%.

The rally is hurting the shorts. Coinglass data in ChainCatcher has Hyperliquid whales at about $4.25 billion short against $3.77 billion long, with shorts down about $167M on paper and longs up about $183M. The 5x ether short at 0x5b5d has gone from a $5.4M gain to a loss of about $8.96M. Mood is still cautious: the Fear and Greed index is only 34, US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $116M on Nov 3 for a third straight session, ether ETFs lost about $85M for a seventh, and Nov 4 data is not yet out. October jobs data lands Nov 6.

Elliptic's Nov 4 update still shows the 18,270 stolen BTC from the Binance hack sitting still. In XRP, Santiment counts 2,147 wallets holding at least 1 million coins, a 2026 high. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again found no large wallet moves; both rose about 6% with the market. The closest signal is a TAO Synergies filing that CoinStats says lifts its holdings above 54,000 TAO.
