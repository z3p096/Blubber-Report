Weekend chop near $67K: Bitcoin is sitting around $67,000 in the early hours of Saturday, down roughly 2.6% on the day after failing to hold $70,000 on Friday. Ether is just above $1,900 and down about 15% on the week, XRP is pinned at $1, and SOL is under $80. The mood is still defensive after hotter US inflation data and steady ETF outflows.

Overnight whale flow leaned toward selling Ether. A wallet tied to the Ethereum Foundation has now sold 5,000 ETH this week, including another 1,000 on Saturday, and a holder who bought ETH over the counter from Galaxy Digital has sent almost all of it back at a loss. On Hyperliquid, one address is holding about $143M in BTC and ETH shorts, even as Glassnode says the biggest traders there flipped net long on bitcoin and dip buyers like 58bro.eth and "7 Siblings" kept scooping up ETH below $2,000.

TAO is holding up best, up about 1% near $215, while HBAR slipped to about six cents. Separate HBAR and TAO searches again found no fresh, verified whale moves. The NEAR price is carried from the last update.
