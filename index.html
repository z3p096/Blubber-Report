Under $67K, XRP under $1: Bitcoin slid below $67,000 early Saturday for the first time since August and is down about 4% on the day near $66,800. Ether is just under $1,900, SOL fell almost 8% to about $75.6, and XRP broke below $1 for the first time since 2024. Traders point to a hot October producer-price report that has markets bracing for a Fed hike in December, a seventh straight day of bitcoin ETF outflows, and money being pulled toward the Anthropic IPO.

Whale flow stayed mostly on the sell side. The Ethereum Foundation-linked wallet and a Galaxy OTC buyer kept dumping ETH, Pump.fun sent another batch of SOL to Kraken, and a 2011-era bitcoin wallet stirred after getting a legal notice. One Hyperliquid address is still sitting on about $143M in BTC and ETH shorts, while dip buyers from earlier in the week are now underwater.

TAO is again the lone gainer, up about 1% near $215, while HBAR sits at about six cents and looks oversold on daily charts. Separate HBAR and TAO searches found no fresh, verified whale moves. The QNT 24h change is carried from the last update.
