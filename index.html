Whales buy the dip: Bitcoin kept sliding into Saturday evening, trading near $63,500 to $63,800, down about 6.6% on the day and roughly 24% below its October high. Ether fell to about $1,745, XRP to about $0.90 and SOL to about $69. The total crypto market is near $2.16 trillion, about $1B in bets were wiped out over 24 hours, and the Fear and Greed index sits at an extreme-fear 13.

The biggest holders are leaning in, not running. CryptoQuant says wallets with more than 1,000 BTC added about 47,000 BTC during the drop, while mid-sized holders with 10 to 1,000 BTC sold close to 65,000. On ether, Lookonchain counts about 95,000 ETH bought by Erik Voorhees-linked wallets and 7 Siblings, and two more big holders pulled about 21,500 ETH off Binance and OKX. Hyperliquid whales remain split between longs and large shorts.

TAO took one of the hardest hits, down about 15% to near $183. Separate HBAR and TAO searches turned up no fresh, verified whale moves.
